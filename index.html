English

Your photo, as a numbered print.

Tiraj prints your photo in one of eleven printmaking techniques: linocut, engraving, risograph, cyanotype and more. Prepare the block, ink it, turn the press, lift the sheet. Every impression comes out a little different, and each one is titled, signed and numbered into its edition. Linocut, risograph and cyanotype are free; Atelier opens all eleven and print-ready 300 dpi files. Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
